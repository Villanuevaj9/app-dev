# My Favorite K-Drama Series

## Twinkling Watermelon (2023) 

### About the K-Drama
*Twinkling Watermelon* is a South Korean fantasy romance drama about a high school student named Eun-gyeol who travels back in time and meets his parents when they were young. Through music and friendship, he discovers more about his family and tries to change the future.

### Why I Like This K-Drama
I like this K-drama because:
- It has a beautiful and meaningful story.
- It shows the importance of family and friendship.
- The music is amazing.
- It has romance, comedy, and emotional moments.
- The actors have great performances.
